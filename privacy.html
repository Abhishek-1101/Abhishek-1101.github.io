Privacy Policy

Snooker Scoreboard (com.abhishek.snookerscoreboard) · Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains what the Snooker Scoreboard app ("the app") does with information. The app is published by Abhishek Singh ("I", "me").

Information I collect

The app has no account or login, and I do not collect your name, email, phone number or location. The player and team names, scores and match history you type in are stored only on your device. I cannot see them.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob (banner ads, an optional rewarded video, and an app open ad). AdMob and its partners may collect and use device information such as your advertising ID, IP address and app usage to show ads and measure them. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later from the privacy option in the app. You can also reset or turn off personalised ads in your device settings.

Learn more: How Google uses data when you use partner sites or apps and the Google Privacy Policy.

Subscription

An optional monthly subscription removes ads. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. I do not see or store your card or payment details. The app only learns from Google Play whether your subscription is active.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and security

I do not sell your data. Information is shared only with the ad and payment services described above. Because match data stays on your device, you can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Changes

I may update this policy and will change the date above when I do.
