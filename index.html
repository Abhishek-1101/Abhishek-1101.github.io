Snooker Scoreboard

A scoreboard for snooker and pool for 2 to 4 players or teams. Add points and minus points, track fouls, frames, breaks and the match clock, declare the match winner by name, and share the result.

  • Custom player or team names
  • Works offline, scores are saved on your phone
  • Display mode for a big screen
